# Gameplay

> *"You're a mercenary pilot, drifting through the outer rim of civilized space. Out here, the only law is what you can enforce with your ship's cannons. The corporations pay well for rust piles and rare metals mined from the asteroid fields, but they don't tell you about the enemy patrols or the void pirates that call these deep space sectors home.*
>
> *Every mission takes you deeper into the unknown. Every asteroid you crack could be your last. But the credits are good, and in this part of the galaxy, that's all that matters. Strap in, pilot. The void awaits."*

## Missions and upgrades

Read the briefing and complete every listed objective to advance. Objectives include destroying ships or asteroids, surviving for a set time, and collecting scrap. Mission five requires defeating the Dreadnought and six enemy ships in solo play. After the initial missions, deep-space sectors continue with scaling objectives and more asteroids.

After each successful mission, enter the Upgrade Bay and spend scrap and rare metal on Reinforced Hull, Weapon Tuning, Engine Overdrive, Magnet Array, or Shield Capacitor. Purchased upgrades, remaining resources, active shields, and pickup timers carry between missions during the run. Health is fully restored to the ship's current maximum at the start of each mission. The base maximum is 150 HP.

## Difficulty

- **Nebula (Easy)** applies a 0.8× damage multiplier and slower enemy spawns. An indicator shows where enemies will enter, and ships stay inside the playfield.
- **Supernova (Normal)** uses standard spawn intervals and damage.
- **BlackHole (Hard)** applies a 1.5× damage multiplier and faster enemy spawns.

Difficulty also changes the distribution of standard [loot drops](loot.md#drop-rates).

## Combat and scoring

- Asteroid collision damage scales with radius at 5 HP per 10 units before the difficulty multiplier. Large asteroids break into smaller fragments when shot. Large asteroids have a 10% chance of spawning as rare asteroids.
- Standard enemy bullets deal 15 base damage. The Dreadnought's cannon and ring projectiles deal 20, and its missiles deal 24. Difficulty scales the damage your ship takes.
- Player shots deal 15 base damage, or 30 with big bullets active. Weapon Tuning increases both. Player shots can intercept enemy bullets, destroying both projectiles on contact.
- Shields absorb damage before hull health. Any damage beyond the shield's remaining HP reaches the hull.
- Asteroids award 100 points each. Enemy ships and the Dreadnought award 10 points per point of maximum HP. See [Enemies](enemies.md) for individual scores. The game automatically [saves your high score](save-data.md) and shows it on the main menu.
